Contact

What to include

Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue. If the issue is visual, a screenshot is helpful. If the issue is related to subscriptions, mention whether you are using a sandbox/test purchase or a production App Store subscription.

Account deletion

Signed-in users can delete their account directly from Momentum under Settings > Account. If you need help, email us from the address associated with your account.

Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are managed by Apple. You can manage or cancel subscriptions from iOS Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

Privacy questions

For privacy questions, data requests, or account deletion assistance, contact the same support email above.
